# Glossary

The town speaks French; the code and the docs speak English. This is the bridge. A term stays in
French in the data and in the interface, and the English here is only how we talk about it.

## The town

- **Maison** — a house: one family, its money, its workshops, its grudges. The unit a player
  controls, and the unit the simulation knows as a place.
- **Ville** — the town, Saint-Aubin. One world, shared by every house.
- **Semaine** — the tick. One game week; 52 make a year.
- **Place** — a seat or a house as something that can be held by an AI or a human.

## The council

- **Charge** — an office. A seat on the council, with a right attached.
- **Maire** — the mayor. Presides, names to small offices, hears appeals.
- **Prévôt** — the provost. Opens investigations, seizes goods, holds the night watch.
- **Échevin** — an alderman. Two of them; they set the ban on grain and wool and keep the
  register of trades.
- **Trésorier** — the treasurer. Levies the cloth tax and opens the accounts.
- **Faveur** — favour: the standing that makes a house electable. Earned by service, lost by
  offence.
- **Prestige** — prestige: what the town thinks of the house, and what caps the residence.
- **Camp** — a faction: houses that vote together, for now.

## The law

- **Ban** — a proclamation fixing a price or forbidding a trade. Enforced from the next day.
- **Greffe** — the clerk's office: acts, complaints, lawsuits, the record of what was decided.
- **Loi** — a law in force, always with the house that carried it.
- **Séance** — a sitting of the council, where the agenda is voted.

## Money and goods

- **Écu** — the coin the player counts in. Stored in the code as integer cents.
- **Denier** — a twelfth of an écu, used for taxes and small fees.
- **Halle** — the market hall, where cloth is sold and where a place must be granted.
- **Drap** — cloth, the good of the opening trade.
- **Laine** — wool, the raw material.
- **Blé** — grain, the good whose price the aldermen fix.
